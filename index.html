Traffic Sandbox

- Driving 0 Waiting at home 0 Arrivals 0/min Trip time vs free flow 1.00x Gave up 0

Homes make trips to the nearest Work by travel time. Streets carry one car per direction per tile, avenues two at double speed. An unsignalled crossing lets one car through at a time; a signal lets a whole axis go. Drag the One-way tool along a road to set its direction, click a one-way tile to clear it. Pan with the Pan tool, right or middle drag, two fingers, or arrow keys and WASD. Zoom with the wheel or the buttons.
