🌿 Road Builder

A calm traffic sandbox. Six districts send cars to one another, each car painted the color of where it's going. Build roads and watch the traffic find its way.

  • Street (30 mph) and Highway (65 mph, two lanes each way): drag to sketch a straight road, then tap Build.
  • Overpass: drag across roads or water. Ramps are added at the ends. Tap the button again to switch between street and highway.
  • Junction: tap an intersection to switch between an all-way stop, traffic lights and a roundabout.
  • Bulldoze removes the top layer. Two fingers pan, pinch to zoom, twist to rotate.
  • Flow shows how close cars get to free-flowing speed.
