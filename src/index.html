🌿 Road Builder

Five towns want to visit each other, but nothing joins them yet. Build the roads and watch the traffic find its way. Each car is painted the colour of the town it is driving to.

Build a highway with an off-ramp

  1. Tap Road and pick Divided highway 2+2.
  2. Tap near one town, then tap near another town. Tap Build.
  3. Pick Ramp. Tap the right-hand carriageway, away from its ends. The ramp leaves along the highway. Move to set where it goes, then Build.
  4. Join each town's gate to the highway with ramps: one onto each carriageway and one off it.

Good to know

  • Red means the road breaks a rule. The bar says which one.
  • ▲ ▼ (or R / F) raise the end you are placing. Above 5 m the road becomes a viaduct that crosses other roads.
  • Starting from a road end continues its direction. With Snap on, the curve turns in 15° steps and its radius in 5 m steps, so 15°, 30° and 45° curves are one drag.
  • Turn (or T) locks the turn at 15° to 90°. Moving then changes only the radius. Curve (or hold Shift) lets you place the bend yourself.
  • Connect joins two road ends. Tap both, then pick Smooth for the widest curve or Corner to run both roads on until they meet. A route across the river becomes a bridge with ramps.
  • Two fingers pan, pinch to zoom and twist to rotate. With a mouse, right drag orbits and middle drag pans.
