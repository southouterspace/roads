Road Builder

🛣️ How to play

Connect homes 🏠 to shops 🏬 with roads. Every finished car trip earns you money, and the town grows along your roads.

  • Road: drag a finger to pave. Crossing water builds a bridge ($40/tile).
  • Bulldoze: drag to clear trees, roads or buildings.
  • Camera: two fingers to pan, pinch to zoom, twist to rotate. Or pick Move to pan with one finger.
  • Buildings with a red ! have no road next to them.
